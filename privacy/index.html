1. About TTAT Analytics

TTAT Analytics is a private, internal analytics utility operated by Thirty Three And A Third for the Thirty Three And A Third YouTube channel. It runs on the operator's own computer and as a job in GitHub Actions, either when the operator starts it or, if the operator turns on the daily schedule, automatically once a day (see section 7). It is not offered as a public service, is not available to other YouTube channels and currently has no users other than the channel's operator.

In this policy, "we" and "the operator" mean Thirty Three And A Third. "You" means a person who authorizes TTAT Analytics with a Google Account, which in practice is the channel's owner.

2. Current status

TTAT Analytics collects live, read-only YouTube channel and analytics data for the Thirty Three And A Third channel when its operator runs it, and stores that data on the operator's Mac. A copy may also be kept in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket so that TTAT Analytics can run in GitHub Actions, including, if the operator turns it on, automatically once a day (see section 7). If the operator turns on email delivery, TTAT Analytics also emails its Performance Report, or a short daily analytics briefing from the automated job, to an address the operator controls (see sections 7–9). It remains a private, internal utility for this one channel; it is not a public analytics service and is not offered to other channels. The storage, refresh and deletion practices described in this policy are in effect.

3. YouTube API Services

TTAT Analytics uses YouTube API Services. By using TTAT Analytics, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Information that Google provides to TTAT Analytics is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. TTAT Analytics is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

4. Information accessed

TTAT Analytics uses Google OAuth 2.0. You sign in on Google's own pages; TTAT Analytics never sees your Google password. TTAT Analytics requests only these two read-only permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
    View your YouTube account information.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
    View YouTube Analytics reports for your content.

TTAT Analytics collects data only for the Thirty Three And A Third channel; a sign-in for any other channel is refused before anything is stored. Using the two permissions above, it accesses and stores:

  • Channel information: the channel ID, name, handle and creation date, and channel statistics such as subscriber, view and video counts.
  • Video information: video IDs, titles, publication times, durations and privacy status, and public statistics such as view, like and comment counts.
  • YouTube Analytics reports: aggregated performance figures for the channel and its videos, by day and by period: views, estimated watch time, average view duration, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained and lost, likes, comments and shares.
  • Audience retention, for videos where YouTube provides it.
  • Aggregated breakdowns by traffic source, country, device type, playback location and subscriber status, where YouTube provides them.
  • Shorts or regular video: each video's classification, taken from YouTube Analytics' own content-type reporting. Videos that YouTube has not yet classified are recorded as unknown.

Video descriptions and tags are intentionally not collected. Not every figure is available for every video or period; figures YouTube does not provide are recorded as unavailable and are never estimated.

YouTube Analytics reports are aggregated statistics. TTAT Analytics does not request or receive personal information about individual viewers, and it does not access the contents of your Google Account beyond the YouTube information described above.

5. How information is used

Information is used only to report on, analyze and visualize the performance of the Thirty Three And A Third channel: for example, comparing episodes and Shorts, following trends over time and understanding subscriber growth, so the channel can make better creative decisions.

TTAT Analytics does not upload, modify or delete YouTube content, and it does not change channel settings. Information from Google is not used for advertising and is not sold. TTAT Analytics does not display advertising and does not allow third parties to serve content.

6. Calculated figures

TTAT Analytics may calculate additional values from YouTube-provided data, for example net subscriber change (subscribers gained minus subscribers lost), watch time in hours and views per day. These values are identified as calculations made by TTAT Analytics, are shown together with the YouTube values they are based on and are never presented as YouTube-provided metrics.

7. Where information is stored

Sign-in credentials

  • On the operator's computer: the OAuth client configuration for TTAT Analytics is kept in a file that only the operator's user account can read, and the refresh token that lets TTAT Analytics renew its read-only access is stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • For jobs in GitHub Actions: the cloud job uses its own, separate OAuth client ("TTAT Analytics Cloud") and a separate refresh token issued for that client. Both OAuth clients belong to the same TTAT Analytics Google Cloud project, and Google treats authorization across the clients of one project as one application: revoking TTAT Analytics' access at Google, for example through the Google Account permissions page or by signing out within TTAT Analytics, can end authorization for both the computer and the cloud job. This client configuration and refresh token are stored in Google Secret Manager in the operator's own Google Cloud project. Only the operator's Google account and the GitHub Actions jobs of the TTAT Analytics source repository can read them. A job places them on its temporary GitHub-hosted machine only while the job runs, in files that only the job can read, and they are discarded with that machine when the job ends.
  • Short-lived access tokens are held in memory while TTAT Analytics runs and are not written to files.
  • Credentials, tokens and client secrets are never included in reports, in emails, in activity logs, in GitHub's job logs or in this website.

YouTube data and reports

Collected YouTube data, and the summaries generated from it, are stored on the local disk of the operator's Mac, in TTAT Analytics' own application data area, in folders that only the operator's macOS user account can open. This data is kept separate from the channel's shared production storage and its normal production backups. It is not stored on this website or on any server operated by Thirty Three And A Third. Apart from the optional email delivery described below, the only other place it is kept is the private cloud storage described in the next subsection.

Cloud storage and automated processing

TTAT Analytics may also keep a copy of its stored data in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket that belongs to the operator's own Google Cloud project, located in the United States. This copy lets TTAT Analytics run as a job in GitHub Actions without the operator's computer, so that its data and reports stay up to date. Jobs run when the operator starts them or, if the operator turns on the daily schedule, automatically once a day. The cloud copy is made of the same kinds of information as the local copy: the YouTube data described in section 4, including video IDs and titles; the operator's own list matching video IDs to episodes; the reports and dashboard generated from that data; activity logs; and, where a job collects new data, the raw YouTube responses from that collection.

  • The bucket is not public. Access is limited to the operator's own Google account and to the GitHub Actions jobs of the TTAT Analytics source repository. Those jobs receive short-lived credentials from Google's Workload Identity Federation for each run; they use no service-account keys or other stored Google Cloud keys.
  • Google encrypts the stored data at rest. While a job runs, its working copy exists only on a temporary GitHub-hosted machine that is discarded when the job ends. TTAT Analytics writes only status messages to GitHub's job logs, not YouTube data or credentials.
  • The bucket never contains OAuth client configurations, refresh tokens or other sign-in credentials, the macOS Keychain, the email service's API key or the email settings, including the recipient address. The computer's credentials and email settings stay on the operator's computer; the cloud job's credentials, the email service's API key and the recipient address for the automated briefing are kept in Google Secret Manager, as described above.
  • Retention and deletion of the cloud copy are described in section 9.

Email delivery (optional)

From the operator's computer. If the operator turns it on, TTAT Analytics can email its Performance Report to an address the operator controls, automatically after a successful collection or when the operator requests it. The report is attached to a short message that names the channel and the collection date. Sending uses Apple Mail and an email account already set up on the operator's Mac; TTAT Analytics does not store any email password or email-account credentials. Only the recipient address is stored, in a settings file on the operator's Mac that only the operator's user account can read. Emailed reports contain channel and video performance information, including video titles. They never intentionally include OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or other authentication material. An emailed copy is transmitted and stored by the email providers involved, in the sender's Sent mailbox and the recipient's mailbox, under those providers' own terms and privacy policies. Email delivery is off unless the operator turns it on, and it can be turned off at any time.

From the automated cloud job. If the operator turns it on, the daily job in GitHub Actions emails a short analytics briefing to the operator's configured address after it has collected data and saved the updated cloud copy. The briefing summarizes recent channel figures, such as the collection date, channel totals and the performance of the newest episode and Shorts, and attaches the Performance Report and dashboard. It is sent from reports@analytics.lyfeisacircus.com through Resend, an email delivery service. The Resend API key and the recipient address are stored in Google Secret Manager, as described above; no email password is stored. Like emailed reports, briefings contain channel and video performance information, and the attached reports include video titles. They never intentionally include OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or other authentication material. Resend transmits each briefing and, according to its documentation, retains email data for 30 days (see Resend's documentation); after delivery, the recipient's copy is stored by the recipient's email provider under its own terms. If an automated run fails or needs the operator's attention, the job may instead send a short status alert, the same way and to the same address. A status alert only says that the automated system needs attention and gives the reason; it contains no YouTube analytics data beyond what a briefing may contain, and never any credentials, tokens or keys. Apple Mail remains the method for emails sent from the operator's computer.

Activity logs

TTAT Analytics keeps activity logs in the same local application data area and, for jobs that run in GitHub Actions, in the private cloud storage described above. They record events such as sign-in checks, the channel ID confirmed during sign-in, collection results and errors. Logs are filtered to exclude credentials and tokens. Email delivery is logged only as sent, failed or skipped, without the recipient address or the message contents.

8. Sharing

  • Information obtained from Google is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers, data brokers or information resellers.
  • AI-assisted analysis. At the operator's own direction, reports generated by TTAT Analytics may be provided to an AI assistant, such as ChatGPT from OpenAI, to help analyze the channel's performance. TTAT Analytics never sends data to an AI service by itself; a report is shared only when the operator chooses to provide it. These reports contain channel and video performance information. They never intentionally include OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or other authentication material. Once a report has been provided to an AI assistant, that copy is handled under the provider's own terms and privacy policy (for OpenAI, see the OpenAI Privacy Policy) and cannot be deleted by TTAT Analytics.
  • Email delivery to the operator. If the operator turns on email delivery, TTAT Analytics sends its Performance Report, or the daily analytics briefing from the automated job, by email to an address the operator controls, along with any status alert about an automated run that fails or needs attention (see section 7). These emails go only to the operator; TTAT Analytics sends no email to anyone else and no marketing or promotional email.
  • Service providers. Google Cloud stores the cloud copy and the cloud job's credentials described in section 7, GitHub runs TTAT Analytics' jobs and hosts its source code, and, if the automated briefing is turned on, Resend delivers it by email. They process this information only to provide those services to the operator, under their own terms and privacy policies (see the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, the GitHub General Privacy Statement, the Resend Data Processing Addendum and the Resend Privacy Policy).
  • Information is not otherwise shared with anyone, except where disclosure is required by law.

9. Retention, refresh and deletion

TTAT Analytics runs when the operator starts it, either on the operator's computer or as a job in GitHub Actions, and, if the operator turns on the daily schedule, automatically once a day as a job in GitHub Actions; it has no other background process. Each time it collects data or checks its sign-in, it applies the rules below, which follow the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, to the copy of the data it works on (local or cloud). So that these checks happen within the policies' time limits, the operator runs TTAT Analytics at least every 30 days while YouTube data is stored, or deletes the stored data.

  • Statistics and analytics results are kept only as long as needed to analyze the channel's performance, and only while authorization remains active. Authorization is confirmed each time TTAT Analytics collects data or checks its sign-in.
  • Titles, publication times, durations and the channel name and handle are refreshed each time data is collected. Copies older than 30 days, including collection summaries that contain titles, are deleted the next time TTAT Analytics collects data or checks its sign-in.
  • Removed videos: each collection checks which videos still exist and deletes stored data about videos that have been removed, including stored API responses that mention them.
  • If sign-in stops working because access was revoked or can no longer be renewed, TTAT Analytics records this. Unless access is restored, it deletes the stored YouTube data the first time it collects data or checks its sign-in at least 7 days later. Because TTAT Analytics is used only by the channel's operator, the operator will also ensure that stored YouTube data is deleted within 30 days of any revocation. The computer and the cloud job each check their own refresh token this way, against the copy of the data that each one works on.
  • Signing out within TTAT Analytics asks Google to revoke its access, removes the saved sign-in from the macOS Keychain and immediately deletes all stored YouTube data, summaries and earlier activity logs from its application data area on the operator's computer. If Google does not confirm the revocation, nothing is removed locally without the operator's confirmation.
  • Deleting data without signing out: TTAT Analytics can immediately delete all stored YouTube data, summaries and earlier activity logs from its application data area while keeping the sign-in. A deletion request sent to the contact address below will be completed within 7 days.
  • Cloud copy. The same rules apply to the cloud copy. In addition, the bucket is configured to delete earlier versions of the stored data after 14 days, reports, dashboards and raw YouTube responses after 27 days, and activity logs after 12 days. Cloud Storage lifecycle processing may cause deletion to occur approximately a day later. The bucket’s soft-delete feature is turned off, so deleted versions are not kept in a recoverable “soft-deleted” state. When signing out, a deletion request or a loss of authorization requires stored YouTube data to be deleted, the operator also deletes the cloud copy, including every retained earlier version, within the same time limits that apply to the local copy, and deletes the cloud job's refresh token from Google Secret Manager.
  • Cloud sign-in. To stop only the automated cloud job, without revoking TTAT Analytics' access for the operator's computer, the operator deletes the cloud job's refresh token from Google Secret Manager or turns off the automated job, or both. The cloud job then stops retrieving YouTube information. This is not a revocation at Google: the authorization remains until it is revoked as described in section 10. The cloud copy remains subject to the rules above.
  • Emailed reports and briefings are copies outside TTAT Analytics' application data area, so signing out, deleting data and the 30-day refresh cannot remove them. Because they contain titles, the operator deletes emailed reports and briefings from the mailboxes they control within 30 days of receipt, and promptly after access is revoked or data deletion is requested. Resend's copy of an automated briefing is kept by Resend under its own retention practice (30 days, according to its documentation, as described in section 7); TTAT Analytics does not delete it.

Records of raw YouTube responses are not changed while they may be kept and are never silently overwritten. Deletions required by these rules are carried out as explicit, logged operations.

10. Revoking access

In addition to TTAT Analytics' own sign-out and deletion procedures described in section 9, you can revoke TTAT Analytics' access to your data at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access there stops TTAT Analytics from retrieving any further information; stored data is then deleted as described in section 9. Because the operator's computer and the automated cloud job use OAuth clients of the same TTAT Analytics project, revoking access there can end authorization for both. To stop only the automated cloud job, see "Cloud sign-in" in section 9.

11. This website

This website contains only static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no JavaScript, and contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party fonts. It does not store anything on your device.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as visitors' IP addresses to operate and secure its service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Thirty Three And A Third does not receive or use that information.
