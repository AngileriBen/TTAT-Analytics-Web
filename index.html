TTAT Analytics

A private analytics utility for the Thirty Three And A Third YouTube channel. It uses read-only YouTube access to report on, analyze and visualize how the channel's episodes and Shorts perform.

What TTAT Analytics does

TTAT Analytics is an internal tool used by the operator of the Thirty Three And A Third YouTube channel. With the channel owner's permission, it uses Google OAuth authorization to retrieve read-only information about the channel and its YouTube Analytics reports.

That information is used only for reporting, analysis and visualization of the channel's performance, such as views, watch time, audience retention and subscriber growth, so the channel can make better creative decisions about future episodes and Shorts.

TTAT Analytics is not a public analytics service. It runs on the operator's own computer, and optionally as a job in GitHub Actions, and stores the data it collects on that computer and, optionally, in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket; if the operator turns on email delivery, its Performance Report is also emailed to an address the operator controls (see the Privacy Policy). It is not offered to other YouTube channels and has no other users.

Read-only access

TTAT Analytics requests only these two read-only Google permissions:

  • youtube.readonly
    View the channel's YouTube account information, such as the channel name, its videos and their public statistics.
  • yt-analytics.readonly
    View YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's content.

What it never does

No changes to YouTube

TTAT Analytics does not upload, modify or delete YouTube videos, playlists, comments, channel settings or any other YouTube content.

No selling or advertising

Data from YouTube is never sold and is never used for advertising.

Current status

TTAT Analytics collects live, read-only YouTube channel and analytics data for the Thirty Three And A Third channel when its operator runs it, and keeps that data on the operator's Mac and, optionally, in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket; if the operator turns on email delivery, its Performance Report is also emailed to an address the operator controls (see the Privacy Policy). No channel analytics are published here: this site provides the public information for the TTAT Analytics OAuth application, including its Privacy Policy.

YouTube API Services

TTAT Analytics uses YouTube API Services. By using TTAT Analytics, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained from Google is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

How TTAT Analytics accesses, uses, stores and shares information is described in the TTAT Analytics Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
